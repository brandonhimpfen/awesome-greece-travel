Greece is a classic Mediterranean destination featuring ancient classical ruins, picturesque island archipelagos, crystal-clear coastal waters, and rugged mountainous terrain. This list is intended for island-hopping travelers, classical history enthusiasts, coastal explorers, and cultural tourists.

## Official & Planning

Essential official government portals, tourist boards, entry guidelines, and archaeological site ticketing.

- [Discover Greece (Official Tourism Portal)](https://www.discovergreece.com/) - Official national travel guide featuring destination ideas, island hopping routes, and cultural guides.
- [Hellenic Ministry of Foreign Affairs - Visas](https://www.mfa.gr/) - Official consular portal for Schengen visa entry rules, passport requirements, and diplomatic missions.
- [Hellenic Organization of Cultural Resources Development (ODAP)](https://etickets.tap.gr/) - Official ticketing portal for booking advance entry passes to the Acropolis, Delphi, Ancient Olympia, and museums across Greece.
- [EMY Weather (Hellenic National Meteorological Service)](https://www.emy.gr/) - Official national weather agency providing regional forecasts, marine winds, and heatwave warnings.

## Transportation & Ferries

Navigating island ferry networks, catamarans, domestic air routes, and mainland rail.

- [Ferryhopper](https://www.ferryhopper.com/) - Comprehensive platform for booking direct and island-hopping ferry routes across the Cyclades, Dodecanese, and Ionian archipelagos.
- [Hellenic Seaways & Blue Star Ferries](https://www.bluestarferries.com/) - Major domestic ferry operators connecting Piraeus, Rafina, and major island ports.
- [Aegean Airlines](https://en.aegeanair.com/) - National flag carrier operating extensive domestic flights linking Athens and Thessaloniki with island airports.
- [Hellenic Train (TrainOSE)](https://www.hellenic_train.gr/) - National passenger rail network connecting Athens, Thessaloniki, and mainland regional lines.

## Destinations & Regions

### Athens & Attica

- [This is Athens (Official City Guide)](https://www.thisisathens.org/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring Plaka, Monastiraki, museums, and neighborhood dining.
- [Acropolis of Athens Official Site](https://odysseus.culture.gr/) - Ministry of Culture portal detailing visiting hours, restoration updates, and archaeological guidelines for the Parthenon.

### The Cyclades (Santorini, Mykonos, Naxos)

- [Santorini Tourism Portal](https://www.santorini.gr/) - Official visitor guide for caldera viewpoints, Oia sunsets, and volcanic beaches.
- [Naxos & Lesser Cyclades Guide](https://www.naxos.gr/) - Regional portal for mountain villages, ancient ruins, and long sandy coastlines.

### The Ionian Islands (Corfu, Lefkada, Kefalonia)

- [Corfu Tourism Board (Kerkyra)](https://www.corfu.gr/) - Official guide for Corfu's Venetian Old Town, fortresses, and coastal trails.
- [Lefkada Official Tourism Portal](https://www.lefkada.gr/) - Travel guide for dramatic western cliffs, turquoise waters, and windsurfing bays.

### Crete & Dodecanese

- [Region of Crete Tourism](https://www.incrediblecrete.gr/) - Comprehensive guide for Chania's Venetian harbor, Heraklion, and Samaria Gorge hiking.
- [Rhodes Medieval City Guide](https://www.rhodes.gr/) - Official visitor portal for exploring the UNESCO-listed medieval walled town and Palace of the Grand Master.

### Mainland Antiquities & Meteora

- [Meteora Monasteries Visitor Guide](https://meteora.com/) - Official traveler portal for visiting the cliff-top monasteries and climbing routes.
- [Delphi Archaeological Site Info](https://odysseus.culture.gr/) - Official guide to the ancient sanctuary, museum, and Mount Parnassus surroundings.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers and remote professionals taking advantage of Greece's Digital Nomad Visa.

- [Greece Digital Nomad Visa (Enterprise Greece)](https://www.enterprisegreece.gov.gr/) - Official government economic agency portal detailing tax incentives, income requirements, and residency application steps.
- [The Loft Coworking Athens](https://www.theloftathens.com/) - Community workspace and hub for remote professionals based in the capital.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contacts, sun protection, and maritime safety.

- [112 European Emergency Number](https://www.gov.gr/) - Direct single European emergency hotline for police, medical, and fire assistance.
- [Greek Tourist Police](https://www.astynomia.gr/) - Dedicated police division offering traveler assistance, legal guidance, and multi-language support.
